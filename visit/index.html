Before you come

A few visiting tips

Come early in spring

From late April through May the benches turn over fast, and the best selection goes early in the week.

Bring measurements and photos

For containers, beds or a tricky shady corner, photos and rough measurements help us steer you to the right plants.

Ask about special orders

If it isn’t on the benches, especially trees and shrubs, ask. We can often order it.

Protect plants on the ride home

In cold months, ask us to sleeve or wrap tropical plants and poinsettias. A few minutes below 50°F can damage them.
