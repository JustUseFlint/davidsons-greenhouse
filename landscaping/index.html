Landscaping FAQ

What landscaping services do you offer in Crawfordsville, IN?

Davidson Greenhouse and Yund Outdoor offer tree and shrub planting, mulch installation, flower bed maintenance, seasonal cleanups, weeding, trimming, landscape design, hardscaping and outdoor property improvements for homes and businesses in Crawfordsville and the surrounding communities.

Do you provide custom landscaping designs?

Yes. Every property is different, so the landscaping team works with you on a plan based on your vision, property layout, budget and how much maintenance you want.

Can you refresh existing flower beds and landscaping?

Yes. The team can clean up overgrown beds, remove weeds, trim shrubs, install fresh mulch and replace tired plants. You don’t have to start from scratch.

Do you install trees and shrubs?

Yes. Trees, shrubs and ornamental plants are installed professionally, with planting techniques that help them establish for the long term.

Do Davidson Greenhouse and Yund Outdoor work together on projects?

Yes. Davidson Greenhouse supplies the plants, trees, mulch and materials, and Yund Outdoor handles landscaping and installation, so one partnership takes you from choosing plants to the finished yard.
