Houseplants & Tropicals

Our houseplant greenhouse is open all year, and it’s one of the largest selections around. You’ll find easy pothos and snake-plant-tough classics, flowering tropicals, trailing vines for hanging baskets, and the odd collector’s plant. Every page lists the light, water and temperature it likes, and whether it’s safe around pets.

Includes: Houseplant, Carnivorous plant.
