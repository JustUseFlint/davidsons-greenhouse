Evolvulus

Evolvulus glomeratus ‘Beach Bum Blue’

Evolvulus has silvery leaves and true-blue flowers that open fresh every morning. It spills nicely from containers and thrives in heat.

  • Great in containers
  • Full sun

Light
Full sun
Water

Hardiness
Grown as an annual in Indiana
Mature size
6–8 in., trailing
Bloom & season
Summer to frost
Soil
Well-drained soil
Spacing
12 in. apart

Growing it in central Indiana

Care & planting tips

  • Flowers close in the afternoon and on cloudy days.
  • Avoid overwatering.
  • Pairs beautifully with yellow and orange flowers.
