In season

The greenhouse changes with the calendar.

We’re open year-round, and the benches turn over with every season. Here’s roughly when to come for what. Selection changes quickly, so call if you’re after something specific.

March – May

Spring

Annuals in packs and pots, hundreds of perennials, hanging baskets, vegetable and herb transplants, seed potatoes, onion sets and plants, and bulk seed by the scoop. Cool-season crops go in early. Tender plants wait for warm soil, usually around mid-May here.

Fall

Garden mums, ornamental cabbage and kale, pumpkins and gourds, plus our annual Fall Festival. Fall is also a great time to plant trees, shrubs and perennials.

Holidays & winter

Christmas trees, wreaths and poinsettias for the holidays, then a greenhouse full of houseplants, succulents and cacti to get you through the gray months.
