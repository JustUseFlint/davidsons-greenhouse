Bring your own container

Drop off the pot. Pick up a planter that blooms all season.

Our “bring your own container” service: leave your planters with us, talk through the look with our in-house designer, and we’ll plant a custom arrangement.

  1. Bring your containers

    Drop off your planters, urns or window boxes at the greenhouse.

  2. Talk it through

    Our in-house designer asks about sun, style and colors, and how much watering you’re up for.

  3. We plant it

    We build a custom arrangement for your pots, made to bloom all season.

  4. Take it home

    Pick it up planted, with care tips for the season.

Arrangements shown are from our photo gallery. Call (765) 364-0556 to ask about timing and pricing for your containers.
